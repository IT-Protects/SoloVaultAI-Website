About Solo Vault AI

We exist because regulated industries cannot accept the risk profile of public AI services. Sensitive data, controlled decisions, and auditability are non-negotiable. Solo Vault was built to deliver agentic capability without the compromise.

Our stance

Public cloud AI is extraordinary technology. It is also the wrong tool for many of the highest-value and highest-risk use cases. When models train on or even transiently process regulated data, the organization inherits risks that compliance teams cannot accept and that auditors will not ignore.

Solo Vault AI takes the opposite approach: keep the models, agents, and data inside a boundary the customer controls. Make human governance explicit. Make every action auditable. Deliver the productivity of agentic systems without the data-exfiltration path.

Private by Design

Architecture that makes data leakage structurally difficult rather than merely prohibited by policy.

Human-Governed

Agents operate under explicit authority. High-stakes actions require human approval.

Production-Ready

Built for real workloads on NVIDIA DGX infrastructure with operational rigor.
