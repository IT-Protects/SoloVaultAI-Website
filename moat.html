GOVERNANCE

MOAT Governance

A deliberate architecture that keeps humans in the decision loop. MOAT is not an afterthought — it is the control plane that makes agentic AI safe for regulated industries.

01

Policy Engine

Declarative rules that define what agents may do, under what conditions, and with which data. Policies are versioned, testable, and auditable.

02

Approval Workflows

High-stakes actions require explicit human sign-off. Escalation paths are configurable by risk level, role, and business context.

03

Immutable Audit

Every agent decision, tool invocation, and human override is recorded in a tamper-evident log suitable for regulators and internal review.

Why “MOAT”?

A moat protects what is valuable. In agentic systems the valuable asset is not only the model weights — it is the decisions those models make on behalf of the organization. MOAT Governance creates a deliberate, enforceable boundary between autonomous capability and uncontrolled action. Humans remain the final authority.
