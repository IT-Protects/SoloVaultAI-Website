Architecture principles

Boundary First

No outbound calls to public model providers for sensitive workloads. All inference stays local or in your dedicated private cluster.

Least Privilege Agents

Every agent receives only the tools, data scopes, and action permissions required for its role. Escalation is explicit and logged.

Auditable by Design

Every decision, tool call, and human interaction is recorded in an immutable trail suitable for regulators and internal audit.
